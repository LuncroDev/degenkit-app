🚀 Quick start

Launch your first token in about five minutes: connect a wallet, fill one form, sign once.

This guide creates a token on Solana. EVM networks work the same way with a slightly different form.

🧰 Before you begin

  • A wallet such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. See Connect your wallet.
  • A little SOL for the service fee and network fees. See Fees.
  • Optional: a square image for your token (PNG or JPG).
Rehearse first

Switch the network to Devnet and mint a test token for free. Devnet SOL has no value and you can get it from a faucet.

📝 Steps

  1. Open the launcher. Go to degenkit.app/launch/solana/.
  2. Connect your wallet. Click Connect wallet and pick yours.
  3. Choose the network. Use Devnet to practice or Mainnet for the real thing.
  4. Fill in the token details. Name, symbol, decimals, total supply, an optional description and image.
  5. Check the summary. The panel shows the network, supply, your wallet and the service fee.
  6. Press to mint. Your wallet opens. Review it and approve.
  7. Wait for confirmation. You'll see a receipt with the mint address and a link to the explorer.

✅ What you get

  • The full supply in your wallet.
  • Mint and freeze authority revoked (unless you turned that off).
  • Locked metadata, so name and symbol can't be edited later.

➡️ Next steps

  • 💧 Make it tradable with Create Liquidity.
  • 📣 Read about DEX listings and marketing.
  • 🔍 Check your token with the Scanner.
