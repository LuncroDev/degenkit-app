**My Tokens** lists the plain and tax tokens you created with DegenKit on the network you're connected to. It reads straight from **on-chain records**, not a third-party balance scan.

## 🛠️ What you can do

- See the tokens you created and their contract addresses.
- For tax tokens: change the buy and sell rates, set the tax receiver, mark the pool address and renounce ownership.

## 💡 Tips

- Switch networks to see tokens you created elsewhere.
- Before renouncing ownership, double-check your tax settings. Renouncing freezes them forever.
