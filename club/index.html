How a call is scored

The same four steps for every call, on every chain.

01

Entry

The token's price in the minute the call was posted. Not the low of the day, not the caller's own entry.

02

Path

The peak, how fast it came, and the share of time the price stayed above entry. A call that halves within an hour is flagged as a fast dump.

03

Checkpoints

The price 1, 6 and 24 hours after the call. These fixed points carry the score, so one wick cannot inflate it.

04

Score

Each call earns -10 to +10 points. An account's score runs 0 to 100 over 90 days and it is ranked after 5 scored calls.

Questions people ask

What is DegenKit Club?

A members-only data service. It reads public posts from tracked X accounts and Telegram channels, picks out the contract addresses they share, and measures what the token's price did from the minute of the post. Each result moves that account's score up or down.

How is a call scored?

Entry is the token's price in the minute the call was posted. We then record the peak, how long the price stayed above entry, and the price 1, 6 and 24 hours later. Those fixed checkpoints carry the score, so a short wick cannot inflate it. A call earns between -10 and +10 points; an account's score runs from 0 to 100 over the last 90 days and it is ranked after 5 scored calls.

Is this financial advice or a signal service?

No. The Club reports what already happened after public posts. It does not tell you what to buy or sell, and past results do not predict future ones. Most tokens promoted on social media lose value.

How do I pay?

From a Solana wallet, in SOL or USDC. The price is $99 for 30 days. There is no card payment and no automatic renewal: when your 30 days end you decide whether to pay again.

Which chains are covered?

Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Robinhood Chain and Elysium. A call is measured on whichever chain the shared contract address trades on.

Can I get a refund?

Payments are on-chain transfers and are final. Access starts as soon as the transaction confirms, so check the sample results on this page before you pay.
