▤ My Tokens

See every token in your wallet and its mint and freeze authority status at a glance.

My Tokens lists the tokens held by your connected wallet and shows whether each token's mint and freeze authority is still active.

🔍 What you see

  • Token name, symbol and balance.
  • A status for mint authority and freeze authority: revoked or still active.
  • A shortcut to manage that token.

🛠️ What you can do from here

  • Revoke an active authority. See Authorities.
  • Burn some of your balance. See Burn Token.
  • Update metadata if it isn't locked. See Update Metadata.

Use this page after any launch to double-check that the authorities are really revoked.
