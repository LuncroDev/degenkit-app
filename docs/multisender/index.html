⇉ Multisender

Send different, custom amounts of a token to many wallets in one go.

Multisender lets you set a different amount for each recipient.

📝 Steps

  1. Open Multisender and select your token.
  2. Paste recipients, one per line, in the format address,amount.
  3. Review totals and confirm. Large lists are sent in a few separate transactions.
9xQeWvG816bUx9EPjHmaT23yvVM2ZWbrrpZb9PusVFin,1000
7vZdQwFhR3sT6nYk2hBp8mE5uLrJ1cXaVq4oGtDsNw9K,250

💸 Fee

0.02 SOL per batch, not per recipient.

✅ Tips

  • Check your CSV for typos before sending. Transfers can't be undone.
  • Make sure your wallet holds at least the total of all amounts.
