Security

Security & transparency

DegenKit is non-custodial. Every action is a transaction you review and sign in your own wallet. Here is exactly what the site does, what it doesn't, and how you can verify it.

What we can and can't do

  • We never ask for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who does is not us.
  • We can't move your funds. Nothing happens without your wallet's signature, and the site holds no custody of tokens or SOL.
  • Mint and freeze authority are revoked by default on Solana tokens, and metadata is locked, so supply can't be increased afterwards.
  • EVM tokens have no mint function. The supply is created once, to your wallet.

What you sign on Solana

  • The Token Creator builds one transaction that creates the mint, your token account, the supply, the metadata and revokes the authorities.
  • The service fee is a plain SOL transfer to our public fee address, shown on the page before you sign:
    A4zUeyAsG1R9JGgqAThryftTZdYDxtEe3fkXhGvkzCPB
  • With most wallets the fee is in the same transaction. With some wallets (such as Solflare) it is sent as a separate small transaction after your token is created.
  • Check what your wallet shows before approving. If something doesn't match this page, reject it.

Open source

  • The website and app code is public so you can read how transactions are built: https://github.com/LuncroDev/degenkit-app
  • Secrets such as storage credentials and the admin passcode live on the server side. They are never in the public code.

Honest limits

  • Not audited. The code has not been audited by a third-party security firm. Test with a small amount first, and use Devnet to rehearse.
  • New site. DegenKit is new, and some wallets may show a warning for new domains. Make sure the address bar says degenkit.app exactly. We are working with wallet teams on reviews.
  • Tokens are your responsibility. DegenKit creates tokens. It doesn't promise value or returns.
