How it works

How to create a token without coding

From idea to a live token in a few minutes. You stay in control the whole time.

  1. Connect your wallet

    Choose a network and connect Phantom, MetaMask or another supported wallet. DegenKit never sees your keys.

  2. Fill in one form

    Enter the name, symbol and total supply. On EVM chains, pick a plain token or a tax token with your own buy and sell rates.

  3. Sign once

    Review the fee and confirm a single transaction. The full supply lands in your wallet and the token is verifiable on the explorer.

FAQ

Token generator FAQ

What is a token generator?+
A token generator, also called a token creator, is a tool that deploys a crypto token from a short form instead of code. You enter the name, symbol and supply, sign one transaction from your own wallet, and the token is created on-chain. DegenKit is a no-code token generator for six networks.
Which networks does DegenKit support?+
Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Robinhood Chain and Elysium. Each has its own launcher: Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Robinhood Chain and Elysium.
Do I need to code anything?+
No. Fill in your token's name, symbol and supply, connect your wallet, and sign one transaction. No Solidity, no Rust, no deploy scripts.
What does it cost to create a token?+
On Solana the Token Creator service fee is 0.28 SOL plus the network fee. On EVM chains the service fee is read from the factory contract and shown on the form before you sign; on Elysium it is 1 HYPE. You also pay the network's gas. See all fees.
Can I create a tax token?+
Yes, on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Robinhood Chain and Elysium. You set separate buy and sell tax rates and the wallet that receives them. Read how buy and sell taxes work.
Is DegenKit non-custodial?+
Yes. DegenKit never holds your keys or funds. Every action is a transaction you review and sign in your own wallet, and the new tokens go straight to that wallet. The code is open source; see the Security page.
Can the supply change after my token is created?+
No. On Solana, mint and freeze authority are revoked and metadata is locked the moment the token is created. On EVM chains the contracts have no mint function, so the supply is fixed forever.
Which wallets does DegenKit work with?+
On Solana: Phantom, Solflare, Backpack and other Wallet Standard wallets. On EVM chains: MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, Trust Wallet, Rainbow and other browser wallets. See supported wallets.
What do I do after creating a token?+
Add liquidity so it can be traded, then share the contract address. The token launch checklist walks through every step from setup to public launch.
Has DegenKit been audited?+
Not yet by a third-party firm. The Solana tools use well-established programs (SPL Token, Token-2022, Metaplex) and the EVM contracts are verified and open source, but we still recommend testing with a small amount first.
What's the difference between Airdrop and Multisender?+
Airdrop sends the same fixed amount to every address on your list. Multisender lets you set a different, custom amount for each recipient.
Where can I get support?+
Email us. The Support page has the address and what to include so we can help faster.
