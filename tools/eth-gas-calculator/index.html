How gas fees are calculated

Every EVM transaction pays gas units × gas price. Gas units measure how much work the transaction does: a simple token transfer uses about 65,000, while deploying a new token contract uses far more. The gas price, quoted in gwei, rises and falls with demand for block space. One gwei is one billionth of an ETH.

That is why the same action costs very different amounts on different networks. Ethereum mainnet has the highest gas prices. Layer 2 networks such as Base and Robinhood Chain usually cost a small fraction of that, and BNB Chain is cheap too. See Ethereum gas fees explained and token launch costs by network.

On Layer 2 networks the wallet may also add a small fee for posting data to Ethereum, which this calculator does not include. Use the wei, gwei and ETH converter if you need to change units.

Questions people ask

How much does it cost to deploy an ERC-20 token?

Gas units for the deployment multiplied by the current gas price, plus any service fee. A token deployment commonly uses around one million gas units, so the cost depends almost entirely on the network and the gas price at that moment.

What is gwei?

A unit of ETH used to quote gas prices. One gwei is 0.000000001 ETH.

Why is Base cheaper than Ethereum?

Base is a Layer 2 network. It processes transactions off Ethereum mainnet and posts compressed data back, so each transaction pays far less.

Where does the live gas price come from?

The page asks each network's public RPC for its current gas price. It changes every block, so refresh before you rely on it.

When is gas cheapest?

Usually when fewer people are using the network, which for Ethereum often means weekends and the hours when both the US and Europe are quiet.
