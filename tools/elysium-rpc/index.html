How to add Elysium to MetaMask

  1. Click Add Elysium to wallet above and approve the request in MetaMask, Rabby or another browser wallet.
  2. Or add it by hand: open your wallet's network settings, choose "Add a custom network" and paste the values from the table.
  3. Switch to Elysium. You need HYPE on Elysium to pay gas.

HYPE reaches Elysium through a bridge from HyperEVM. Read how to bridge HYPE to Elysium, Elysium gas fees and what Elysium is.

Questions people ask

What is the Elysium chain ID?

99901, which is 0x1863d in hexadecimal.

What is the Elysium RPC URL?

The public RPC used by DegenKit is https://rpc.elysium.kinetiq.xyz. Check Kinetiq's documentation for the current official list.

What token pays gas on Elysium?

HYPE. You need a small amount of HYPE on Elysium to send any transaction.

Is Elysium an EVM chain?

Yes. Elysium is EVM-compatible, so MetaMask, Rabby and other EVM wallets work, and tokens are standard ERC-20 contracts.

How do I get HYPE on Elysium?

Bridge it from HyperEVM using the bridge linked from Kinetiq's official channels.
