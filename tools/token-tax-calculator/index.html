How token taxes add up

A tax token takes a percentage of every buy and every sell and sends it to a wallet chosen by the creator. The two taxes compound: with a 5% buy tax and a 5% sell tax, a holder loses 9.75% on a round trip, not 10%, because the sell tax applies to what is left after the buy tax.

That is why the break-even number matters. At 5% and 5%, the price has to rise about 10.8% before a buyer gets their money back, before gas and pool fees. High taxes make a token hard to trade, and buyers check for them. Read how buy and sell taxes work and how to spot a honeypot.

This calculator ignores slippage, pool fees and gas. Use the liquidity calculator for price impact and the gas calculator for network costs.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate buy and sell tax on a token?

Multiply the amount by one minus the tax rate. A 1,000 USD buy with a 5% buy tax leaves 950 USD of tokens. Selling 950 USD with a 5% sell tax returns 902.50 USD.

What is a reasonable token tax?

It varies by project. Many buyers avoid tokens with a combined tax far above 10%, and very high sell taxes are a common warning sign of a honeypot.

Do the buy and sell tax add together?

Not exactly. They compound. A 5% buy tax and a 5% sell tax cost 9.75% in total on a round trip.

Can a tax be changed after launch?

It depends on the contract. With a DegenKit tax token the owner can change rates within the contract's limits, or renounce ownership to lock them.

Does this include gas and slippage?

No. It only models the token's own buy and sell tax.
