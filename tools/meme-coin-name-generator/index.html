What makes a good meme coin name

The names that stick are short, easy to say and easy to spell. If someone hears the name once in a voice chat, they should be able to type it into a search bar. Two words is usually the limit, and the ticker should be three to five letters.

Check three things before you commit. First, search the name on a token explorer and on X: a clash with an existing token confuses buyers and helps copycats. Second, avoid real brands, companies and people, because trademark owners do act against tokens that use their names. Third, say it out loud and make sure it does not mean something unfortunate in another language.

Questions people ask

How do I come up with a meme coin name?

Start from a theme people already enjoy, such as an animal or a joke, keep it to one or two words and make sure the ticker is short. Then check that no existing token already uses it.

How long should a ticker symbol be?

Three to five letters is the norm. Short tickers are easier to remember and to search for.

Can I use a brand or celebrity name?

You should not. It can infringe trademarks or publicity rights and exchanges and explorers may delist or flag the token.

Can I change the name after creating the token?

On an ERC-20 the name and symbol are fixed in the contract at deployment. Choose carefully before you sign.
