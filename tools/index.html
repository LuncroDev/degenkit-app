What you can do with these tools

Launching a token is only the first step. After that you need to keep your wallet safe, connect to the right network and get tokens into holders' hands. These tools cover those jobs. The token approval checker lists every ERC-20 allowance your wallet has granted and lets you revoke the ones you no longer trust. The Robinhood Chain RPC page gives you the chain ID, endpoints and explorer in one place, with a button that adds the network to your wallet.

When you are ready to create a token, the token creator works on six networks from one short form. New to this? Start with the token launch checklist, learn how ERC-20 approvals work, or read wallet security for token creators.

Questions people ask

Are these crypto tools really free?

The approval checker and the Robinhood Chain RPC page are free to use. Reading data costs nothing. If you choose to revoke an approval, you pay only the network gas for that transaction, straight from your own wallet.

Do I need to connect a wallet?

Not to look things up. You can paste any address into the approval checker. A wallet is only needed when you decide to sign something, such as revoking an approval or adding a network.

Are the tools non-custodial?

Yes. DegenKit never holds your keys or funds. Every action is a transaction you review and sign in your own wallet.

Which networks do the tools cover?

The approval checker works on Ethereum, Robinhood Chain, Base and BNB Chain. The Solana tools (Multisender, Airdrop and Scanner) live inside the Solana launcher.
