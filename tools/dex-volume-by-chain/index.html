How to read DEX volume when you choose a chain

DEX volume is the dollar value of swaps on decentralized exchanges. A chain with high volume has more traders, deeper pools and more tools that already support it, so a new token there is easier for buyers to find and trade.

In the snapshot above, Solana and Ethereum are close at the top, and the EVM networks together (Ethereum, Base, BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain) carry well over twice Solana's volume. Uniswap is the largest single DEX and runs on Ethereum, Base and Robinhood Chain, which means one ERC-20 skill set covers three of the five biggest markets.

Volume is not the whole picture. Most of it comes from large established tokens and stablecoins, so it does not tell you how many new tokens succeed. Gas cost matters too: Ethereum has the deepest liquidity and the highest fees, while Base, BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain are far cheaper to launch and trade on. Questions people ask

Which blockchain has the most DEX volume?

It changes month to month. In mid-November 2026 DefiLlama showed Solana and Ethereum at the top for 30-day volume, followed by Base, BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain.

Does high DEX volume mean I should launch there?

It is one signal. High volume means more traders and liquidity, but also more competition and, on Ethereum, higher gas costs.

Where does this data come from?

DefiLlama, which aggregates swap volume reported by decentralized exchanges on each chain.

What is the biggest DEX?

Uniswap had the largest share of tracked DEX volume in November 2026, with pools on Ethereum, Base and Robinhood Chain.

Is Elysium's volume low because it is new?

Yes. Elysium's mainnet launched in November 2026, so it has only days of history compared with years for the others.
