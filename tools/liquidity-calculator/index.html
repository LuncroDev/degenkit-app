How the liquidity calculator works

A Uniswap V2-style pool holds two assets and keeps their product constant. The launch price is simply the amount of ETH (or BNB, HYPE, SOL) you add divided by the number of tokens you add. Multiply that price by the total supply and you get the starting market cap, also called fully diluted valuation.

The price impact figure shows how far one buy moves the price. A thin pool means small buys cause big swings, in both directions. If a 0.1 ETH buy moves the price by more than 15%, the pool is probably too shallow for a public launch.

Keep these in mind: tokens you leave outside the pool still count toward market cap, the pool fee is taken from every swap, and the ETH you add is at risk like any other liquidity position. Read how to add liquidity on Uniswap and market cap vs FDV for the details.

How much liquidity should you add?

There is no single right number. More liquidity gives buyers a steadier price and makes the token harder to manipulate, but it is your own money in the pool. Use the calculator to compare two or three setups, then check the price impact of the buy sizes you expect from your community.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate a token's launch price?

Divide the amount of ETH (or other paired coin) you add to the pool by the number of tokens you add. With 2 ETH and 800,000,000 tokens, the launch price is 0.0000000025 ETH per token.

How is the starting market cap calculated?

Launch price multiplied by total supply. This is the fully diluted value, because it counts every token, including any you keep outside the pool.

What is price impact?

The amount a single trade moves the pool price. It depends on the trade size compared with the liquidity in the pool. Deeper pools have lower price impact.

Does this work for Base, BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain?

Yes. Any constant-product pool uses the same maths, whether it is Uniswap V2 on Ethereum or Base, PancakeSwap V2 on BNB Chain or a Raydium CPMM pool on Solana.

Is this financial advice?

No. The calculator only does arithmetic on the numbers you enter. Token launches are high risk and you can lose the liquidity you add.
