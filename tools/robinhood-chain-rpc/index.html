Test the RPC yourself

Ask the endpoint for its chain ID. A healthy mainnet RPC answers 0x1237 (4663).

curl -s https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"eth_chainId","params":[]}'

Questions people ask

What is the Robinhood Chain RPC URL?

The public mainnet RPC is https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com with chain ID 4663. The public testnet RPC is https://rpc.testnet.chain.robinhood.com with chain ID 46630.

Is the public Robinhood Chain RPC free?

Yes, but Robinhood describes its public endpoints as rate-limited and not recommended for production. For apps, bots or indexers, use a provider such as Alchemy, QuickNode, Chainstack, dRPC or your own node.

What is Robinhood Chain's chain ID?

Mainnet is 4663 (0x1237). Testnet is 46630.

What currency pays gas on Robinhood Chain?

ETH on Robinhood Chain. ETH on Ethereum mainnet has to be bridged first.

Is there a public WebSocket RPC?

Robinhood's docs list WebSocket endpoints through providers such as Alchemy. The public wss addresses are sequencer feeds for nodes, not a general WebSocket RPC.
