If you search for Robinhood Chain tokens, one name keeps coming up: **Pons**. It is a token launchpad, often called "pump.fun for Robinhood Chain", and it became one of the chain's busiest apps within weeks of launch. This guide explains what Pons is, how it works, the risks, and how it differs from creating your own token.

DegenKit is not affiliated with Pons or with Robinhood. This page is an independent explainer based on public sources, listed at the end. Details such as fees can change, so check Pons directly before you use it.
:::

## What is Pons?

Pons is a **non-custodial token launchpad** built for Robinhood Chain. Anyone can create a fixed-supply token from their own wallet, with no code, and the token can be traded straight away. Public coverage describes it as a launcher for tokens with a **fixed supply of 1 billion**, placed directly into a trading pool ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/resources/what-robinhood-chain-ethereum-layer-2-network-tokenized-stocks?amp=1)).

Robinhood Chain itself is an Ethereum Layer 2 built on Arbitrum, with ETH as the gas token. Its public mainnet opened on July 1, 2026, and Pons went live there in mid-July ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/pons-token-robinhood-chain-pump-fun-rival), [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/markets/pons/)).

## What is PONS, the token?

**PONS** is the token associated with the Pons launchpad. It is not issued by Robinhood Markets, and it is not Robinhood stock or a tokenized version of it ([KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/pons-token-robinhood-chain-pump-fun-rival)). Reports describe a model where a share of the launchpad's fees funds buybacks of PONS ([Datawallet](https://www.datawallet.com/it/crypto/pons-explained)). Treat PONS like any other volatile token: its price can move sharply in both directions.

## How the Pons launchpad works

1. You connect a wallet on Robinhood Chain.
2. You enter a name, symbol and image.
3. You pay a launch fee and sign one transaction. One source reports a flat fee of about 0.0005 ETH ([AirdropAlert](https://airdropalert.com/?p=324531)), but confirm the current fee on the site.
4. The token is created with a fixed supply and starts trading.

Several sources also say launched tokens' liquidity is locked by the protocol. That protects against one kind of rug pull, but it does **not** tell you whether a token is a good project.

## Risks to know before you buy a Pons token

- **Anyone can launch.** Permissionless means most launches are copies, jokes or scams. Check the contract and the deployer before you buy.
- **Locked liquidity is not a guarantee.** Holders can still lose everything when a token's price goes to zero.
- **Competition changes things fast.** In early August, Uniswap launched its own launchpad with zero fees and took a large share of launch volume ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/377349/pons-robinhood-chain?amp=1)). Launchpad rankings shift weekly.
- **Scam reports exist.** Crypto Briefing has linked a batch of Robinhood Chain launches to an alleged rug-pull group ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/markets/pons/)).

Never buy only because a token trades on a popular launchpad.
