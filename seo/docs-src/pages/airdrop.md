**Airdrop** sends one fixed amount to every address on your list.

## 📝 Steps

1. Open **Airdrop** and select your token.
2. Enter the **amount per wallet**.
3. Paste recipient addresses, **one per line**. Extra columns are ignored.
4. Review the summary and confirm. Large lists are split into a few transactions, and you sign each one.

## 💸 Fee

**0.02 SOL per batch**, not per recipient, plus network fees.

## ✅ Tips

- Test with 2 or 3 addresses first.
- Remove duplicates so nobody receives twice.
- Keep enough SOL for the network fees, including new token accounts for recipients.

Need different amounts per person? Use [Multisender](/docs/multisender/). Not sure which? Read [Airdrop vs Multisender](/blog/airdrop-vs-multisender/).
