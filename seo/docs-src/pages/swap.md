**Swap** finds the best route across Solana DEXs using the **Jupiter** aggregator.

## 📝 Steps

1. Open **Swap**.
2. Choose the token you give and the token you receive, and enter an amount.
3. Pick a **slippage** setting: 0.5%, 1% or 3%. The default is 0.5%.
4. Review the quote and confirm in your wallet.

## 💸 Fee

**0.01 SOL** plus the network fee, collected in a separate small transaction.

## ⚠️ Good to know

- Quotes come from Jupiter, a third-party service DegenKit doesn't control.
- Prices can move between quote and execution. High slippage means you accept a worse price.
- Learn the difference in [Slippage vs price impact](/blog/slippage-vs-price-impact/).
