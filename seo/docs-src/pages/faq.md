## What is DegenKit?

A no-code, non-custodial token launcher for Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base and Robinhood Chain.

## Do I need to know how to code?

No. Fill in a form and sign with your wallet.

## Does DegenKit hold my funds or keys?

No. Everything is signed in your own wallet.

## How much does it cost?

On Solana the Token Creator costs 0.28 SOL plus network fees. See [Fees](/docs/fees/) for every tool and network.

## Can the supply change after creation?

No. Solana tokens have mint authority revoked by default, and EVM tokens have no mint function.

## Can I change the name or image later?

Not for tokens created with DegenKit: metadata is locked on Solana, and EVM names and symbols are permanent.

## Is DegenKit audited?

Not by a third-party firm. Test with a small amount first and rehearse on Devnet.

## Can I undo a transaction?

No. Blockchain transactions are final. Always review before you sign.

## Does DegenKit guarantee my token will be valuable or listed?

No. DegenKit creates tokens. Liquidity, listings and marketing are up to you. See [Token launch checklist](/blog/token-launch-checklist/).

## Where do I get help?

[Support](/support/) or support@degenkit.app.
