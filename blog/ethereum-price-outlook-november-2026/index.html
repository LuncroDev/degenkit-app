Where Is Ethereum Heading? ETH After the November 2026 Crash

ETH fell toward $2,200 as ETF outflows, $210 million in liquidations and a delayed upgrade landed in the same week. The levels traders are watching, the data behind the drop and what could turn it around.

DegenKitNovember 7, 2026 · 4 min read

Ethereum had one of its worst weeks of the year. ETH lost more than 8% in a single day on November 6, falling toward $2,200, and was trading near $2,240 on November 7. That's about 55% below its August 2025 high near $4,950.

Here's what pushed it down, the levels traders are watching and what could change the picture.

Figures as of November 6–7, 2026

This is market news, not financial advice. Prices and levels change quickly.

Why ETH fell

1. ETF money left. US spot Ether ETFs had net outflows of nearly $100 million on November 5, led by $75.2 million from BlackRock's ETHA. Another $93.3 million left on November 6 (crypto.news, KuCoin News).

2. Leverage unwound. More than $210 million of ETH positions were liquidated in 24 hours, $179 million of them longs. Open interest in ETH futures dropped to about $11.4 billion, down from about $16 billion in late September.

3. The upgrade was delayed. On November 6 the Ethereum Foundation said developers would delay activating the Glamsterdam upgrade on the Hoodi test network. Security firm ChainSecurity found that two gas-accounting changes, EIP-8037 and EIP-8038, could break eight assumptions that existing smart contracts rely on. No new date has been set (KuCoin News).

4. Everything else fell too. Tech stocks sold off on November 5, and crypto went with them. See why crypto crashed in early November.

The levels traders are watching

  • Support: about $2,200 first, then $2,100 and $2,000. A clean break below $2,200 would put those lower levels in play.
  • Resistance: about $2,350, then the $2,450–$2,500 zone, where the last recovery attempt failed.
  • Momentum: the daily RSI was about 38, below neutral but not yet oversold.

These come from the analysis cited by crypto.news. They're areas where buyers or sellers have shown up before, not promises.

What could turn it around

There's one piece of good news in the data. ETH held on centralized exchanges has fallen to about 14.6 million ETH, from more than 21 million in early 2025. Fewer coins on exchanges usually means fewer coins ready to sell quickly.

Other signals to watch:

  • ETF flows turning positive for several days in a row.
  • A new Glamsterdam date once the security review with ChainSecurity and Dedaub is done.
  • Bitcoin holding $75,000. ETH rarely recovers on its own while Bitcoin is falling.

What it means for building on Ethereum

Gas on Ethereum is paid in ETH, so a lower ETH price makes the same transaction cheaper in dollars. If you were waiting to create an ERC-20 token, creation costs less in dollar terms now. The trade-off is a quieter market with fewer buyers. Our guide to launching during a crash covers that side.

Wrap up

ETH is near $2,240, with $2,200 as the line most traders are watching and $2,350 as the first hurdle above. ETF flows, the Glamsterdam timeline and Bitcoin will decide the next move. If you're building on Ethereum, DegenKit's Ethereum launcher is ready when you are.
