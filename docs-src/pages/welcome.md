DegenKit is a **no-code, non-custodial token launcher**. You fill in a form, your own wallet signs, and your token exists on-chain. No Solidity, no Rust, no deploy scripts. 🎉

This documentation explains every tool, step by step, in plain language.

## ✨ What you can do

| Network | What's available |
|---|---|
| ◎ **Solana** | Token Creator, My Tokens, Authorities, Update Metadata, Burn, Airdrop, Multisender, Scanner, Vanity Address, Swap, Create & Remove Liquidity |
| ⬡ **Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Robinhood Chain** | Token Creator (fixed supply), Tax Token (custom buy/sell tax), My Tokens |

## 🧭 Where to start

- 🚀 New here? Read the [Quick start](/docs/quick-start/) and launch your first token in about five minutes.
- 👛 Need help connecting? See [Connect your wallet](/docs/wallets/).
- 💸 Want to know the costs? See [Fees](/docs/fees/).
- 🔒 Want to know what we can and can't do? See [Security](/docs/security/).

## 🛡️ Principles

- **Non-custodial.** Your keys stay in your wallet. DegenKit never holds tokens or funds.
- **One clear signature.** You see what you sign before you sign it.
- **No hidden powers.** Solana tokens are created with mint and freeze authority revoked and metadata locked by default. EVM tokens have no mint function.
- **Honest limits.** DegenKit has not been audited by a third-party firm. Start small and rehearse on Devnet.
