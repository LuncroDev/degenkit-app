The EVM Token Creator makes a **fixed-supply** token and sends the whole supply to your wallet.

## 📝 Steps

1. Open your network: [Ethereum](/launch/ethereum/), [BNB Chain](/launch/bnb/), [Base](/launch/base/) or [Robinhood Chain](/launch/robinhood/).
2. Connect your wallet. If it's on another network, accept the switch request.
3. Fill in **name**, **symbol** and **total supply**.
4. Review the summary, including the service fee.
5. Sign the transaction and wait for confirmation.

## 🔒 What you get

- **No mint function, ever.** The supply is created once, in this one transaction.
- **18 decimals**, the standard for ERC-20 tokens.
- Name and symbol are permanent.

## 💸 Fees

You pay network gas plus the service fee shown on the form. The service fee is read from the factory contract, so the amount you see is the amount charged. See [Fees](/docs/fees/).
