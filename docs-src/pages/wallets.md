DegenKit works with the wallet you already use. Nothing is installed and no account is created.

## ◎ Solana wallets

DegenKit uses the **Wallet Standard**, so any compatible wallet appears in the menu automatically.

| Wallet | Notes |
|---|---|
| 👻 **Phantom** | Detected automatically when the extension is installed |
| 🔥 **Solflare** | Detected automatically. Fee is collected in a separate small transaction |
| 🎒 **Backpack** | Detected automatically |
| 🦊 **MetaMask** | Works when Solana is enabled in MetaMask |
| Others | Bitget, Coinbase Wallet, Trust Wallet and Magic Eden are listed too, if they support Solana |

Click **Connect wallet**, choose a wallet marked **Detected**, and approve the connection.

## ⬡ EVM wallets

On Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base and Robinhood Chain, connect MetaMask or any browser wallet that supports EVM. If your wallet is on a different network, the page asks it to switch.

## 🔧 If your wallet isn't detected

1. **Refresh the page** after installing or unlocking the wallet.
2. **Unlock the extension.** Locked wallets may not announce themselves.
3. **Check the browser.** The extension must be enabled for this site.
4. **Disable other wallet extensions** if two of them fight over the same browser slot.
5. Still stuck? See [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/).

:::warning Never share your seed phrase
DegenKit will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. If any site, DM or email does, it's a scam.
:::
