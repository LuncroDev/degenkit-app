**Remove Liquidity** gives you back your share of a pool.

## 📝 Steps

1. Open **Remove Liquidity**.
2. Your positions appear in a list, found from the LP tokens in your wallet. You can also look up a pool by its **pool ID**.
3. Choose how much to remove: **25%, 50%, 75% or 100%**.
4. Adjust slippage if needed. The default is 1%.
5. Confirm in your wallet.

## 💸 Fee

**0.02 SOL** plus network fees, collected in a separate small transaction.

## ⚠️ Good to know

- You get your **share of the pool**, which usually isn't the same amounts you put in.
- Removing liquidity makes trading harder for buyers. Do it openly and tell your community.
- To lock liquidity instead, see [How to lock liquidity on Solana](/blog/lock-liquidity-on-solana-burn-and-earn/).
- More detail: [How to remove liquidity](/blog/how-to-remove-liquidity/).
